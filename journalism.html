📚 Five learning sets

Each set includes a theory summary and 30 questions based on Bloom’s Taxonomy levels C3–C6. Total: 150 questions.

🎯 Learning objectives

  • C3 · Apply: apply journalistic principles to real-world situations.
  • C4 · Analyze: analyze problems, structures, and language choices.
  • C5 · Evaluate: evaluate accuracy, ethics, and editorial decisions.
  • C6 · Create: create headlines, leads, interview questions, or copy.

📖 Before you roll

Read the theory summary for your selected set. During the game, each turn presents one question. A correct answer moves the player forward by the dice number; an incorrect answer keeps the token in place.

How to win

Be the first player to reach square 100 with an exact dice roll. Snakes move your token down, while ladders help you climb up.

Scoring

Correct answer: +10 points. Incorrect answer: +0 points. Practical challenges can be assessed by peers or the lecturer using the provided rubric.
